How it works

Open a group and Orion’s vertical tab sidebar. Auto reads that group, asks AI for an order, checks the captured tree, and applies it. Advanced stops at a tree review before Apply.

Native objects, pins, navigation history and selection are guarded. Undo restores the original tree; Keep this order ends Undo. Never repeat an operation with an unknown outcome: reconnect and inspect Recovery.

Experimental integration for Orion 1.1.3 (152), macOS. Other builds and restart scenarios need separate verification.
